## About

I am an atmospheric scientist investigating the physical processes that govern tropical circulation, organized convection, precipitation, and climate variability. My research focuses on Mexico, Mesoamerica, the Caribbean, and tropical South America.

I combine atmospheric dynamics with observations, reanalysis datasets, numerical simulations, and scientific computing to understand hydroclimate variability and predictability, from subseasonal fluctuations to interannual climate variations. I am also interested in high-impact atmospheric phenomena, particularly thunderstorms and lightning.

## Research areas

- **Tropical dynamics and convection** — Convectively coupled equatorial waves, tropical circulation, and organized convection.
- **Climate variability** — El Niño–Southern Oscillation (ENSO), Madden–Julian Oscillation (MJO), and Quasi-Biennial Oscillation (QBO).
- **Subseasonal-to-seasonal predictability** — Rainfall variability, rainy-season characteristics, and the Mesoamerican midsummer drought.
- **Regional hydroclimatology** — Atmospheric moisture transport, low-level jets, and regional precipitation patterns.
- **Atmospheric hazards** — Thunderstorms, lightning climatology, and their societal impacts.
